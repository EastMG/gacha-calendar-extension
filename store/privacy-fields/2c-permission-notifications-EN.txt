notifications

Used only for **deadline reminders**: before a banner or event ends (lead times of 72 / 24 / 6 / 2 hours,
and for one item only its most urgent due lead time is delivered), the extension shows a system
notification saying which game/item is ending and how many hours are left.

Key points:
1) Can be switched off in the settings page ("提醒与导出 -> 到期提醒"). When off, no notifications are shown
   at all. Lead times are four toggle chips (72/24/6/2 hours); clearing all of them also stops reminders.
2) The settings page also has a "send test notification" button: it shows one notification **only when the
   user clicks it**, so users can verify that the OS / Do Not Disturb does not block Chrome notifications.
   It never touches real reminders and stores no dedupe state.
3) No network access: notification text comes entirely from the local cache (lastData in
   chrome.storage.local); no requests are made.
4) No page access: the extension has no content script.
5) Rate-limited: each item notifies once per lead time (deduped by item + date range + direction + lead);
   when several lead times are due at once they are collapsed into a single notification.
